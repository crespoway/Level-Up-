Level 1

Job + your own space

A W-2 job is a regular job where taxes come out of your check.

That check is your first superpower.

Why? Because landlords and banks want to see steady pay.

  • Get a steady job. Show up. Keep it.
  • Use your pay stubs to rent a room in a calmer neighborhood.
  • Try to keep rent to about a third of your pay or less.
ChaosNoise, stress, no sleep. Hard to think.
PeaceQuiet room. Clear head. Now you can plan.

Peace → Focus → Momentum. That's how it started for me.

Boss trap

Never send a deposit for a room you haven't seen in person. Room scams are everywhere.

You have steady pay and a place where you can sleep and think.

Level 2

Read your report card

Your credit report is like a report card for money. Banks read it before they say yes or no.

You can't fix what you never read.

Where to get it

AnnualCreditReport.com — free, all three reports. That's the real one.

Look for anything bad, like collections (old bills a company is chasing). Next to each one, write one word: right or wrong.

  • Wrong? Challenge it. It's free. They have to prove it or remove it.
  • Right? Ask them to remove it if you pay. Get that promise in writing first.
Boss trap

"Just settle it" can backfire. It may barely help your score. And paying on a very old debt can restart it in some states. Look up your state first.

Nothing bad on your report? You're ahead. Skip to Level 3.

Kenny:I'll pay if you remove it from my report. In writing first.

Level 3

Build your credit

Big banks mostly look at your numbers. A credit union is a smaller bank owned by its members, and it can also see how you handle your money there.

  • Join a credit union. Send your paycheck there.
  • Open a secured card. You put down a deposit, like $200–$300. That's your limit.
  • Put one small bill on it, like your phone.
  • Pay it off in full every month.

Level 4

Business the right way

Now you can open an LLC, which is a simple business registration. Look up your state's real cost first. Never use rent money.

Secret: you have TWO report cards.

YouYour personal credit. Levels 2–3.
Your businessA totally separate report card.

Paying business bills does not raise your personal score. Different scoreboards.

To build business credit, open supply accounts that let you pay in 30 days. Before you open one, call and ask:

You

"Do you report my payments to the business credit bureaus? Which ones?"

  • Pay early, not just on time.
  • Start with two accounts, not six.
Boss trap

Most business credit cards make you promise to pay if the business can't. An LLC won't protect you from that.

Scene: six months later
Kenny:I paid my supply accounts early for 6 months!
Lender:I checked your business file. It's blank.
Kenny:How?!
Lender:Your supplier reports somewhere else. Or not at all.
AHA!Paying perfectly means nothing if nobody is keeping score. Call first, then open.
Level clear when…

Your business has its own name, bank account, and two accounts reporting.

Power move: Call one supplier. Ask the question. Write down the answer.

Level 5 · Boss level

Make money. Go free.

Learn one real skill. For me, it was reselling: buy things cheap, sell them for more.

  • Start small with your own money.
  • Track every dollar in and out.
  • Put profit back into the business.
When to leave the W-2

Don't quit on a good month. Quit when your business pays your bills for several months in a row and you have savings to fall back on.

Final boss: scams

Skip "credit privacy numbers," fake income on applications, and "secret" credit tricks. They're fraud or they don't work. The real path is slower, and it's real.
